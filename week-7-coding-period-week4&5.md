## GSoC Coding Period Update

First of all, sorry for the delay in this week's blog.

This week was probably one of the most challenging and hectic weeks of my GSoC journey so far.

A major part of the week went into refining the KEP. It almost felt like an infinite loop:

Feedback → Revise the KEP → Discussion → More Feedback → Repeat.

But that's exactly how good designs are built. Every discussion helped us refine the proposal further, and now we're finally approaching the last stage.

Alongside the KEP, I also implemented the lifecycle APIs, prepared demos for our mentor meetings, and continued validating the workflow before moving ahead with the next implementation steps.

One thing I really appreciate is how involved my mentors have been throughout this process. Over the past couple of weeks, we've spent hours discussing approaches, reviewing designs, and iterating together. Their feedback has helped shape both the API design and my way of thinking about software engineering.

The reason we had to revisit the KEP multiple times was that our API structure evolved as implementation progressed. Once the design changes, the documentation has to evolve with it as well. It takes time, but getting the design right is always worth the effort.

On another note, I also applied for the Amazon ML Summer School and I'm happy to share that I qualified for the third round — the Online Assessment. Hoping for the best!

Now everything is back on track, so hopefully no more delays in my weekly updates. 😄

Every week of GSoC teaches me something new. Beyond writing code, I'm learning how production features are designed, how proposals are reviewed, how feedback shapes better software, and how collaboration works in a large open-source project.

And honestly, knowing that the code I'm writing can eventually become part of a project used by people around the world is an amazing feeling.

To everyone preparing for GSoC next year: all the best! Stay consistent, contribute because you enjoy learning, and keep asking questions. I'll continue sharing my journey and everything I'm learning along the way.
